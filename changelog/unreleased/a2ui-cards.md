### New

- **Cards:** agents can put an interactive A2UI card in the chat

  A card is a small form, a choice to make, a status board the agent keeps up to date in place, or pictures, video and sound, drawn in Geniro's own style. What you type is kept until you press the card's button, except password fields, which are never saved. The press reaches the agent as your message, team members' cards included. If the agent rewrites a field you had changed, its value wins and the field is marked so you see it.
