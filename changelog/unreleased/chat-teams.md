### New

- **Teams:** have a team of agents do a job right in your chat, without building a team first

  Ask your chat for a team ("start the architecture work, build a team"): its agent asks what it needs, then shows a card with the plan, itself as the manager, and the agents it wants, each with its role, its own task, CLI and model. Press **Start** and the same chat becomes that team, keeping the conversation; a note typed on **Start** is applied at once. Later ask it to add, remove or change an agent and a card shows every change before and after; **Apply** changes the running team, and a removed agent finishes its current call first. The same works on a running library team, for that run only. Such a team is never saved to your library, and its chat shows "team · N agents" in the list.
