### New

- **Command center:** move a whole project block by dragging its header

  The block's cards and open threads move with it, it stays where you dropped it on this device, and Reset layout puts it back.

### Improved

- **Command center:** dragging a card inside its project block shows where it will land

  The other cards make room around a dashed slot; drop the card there to reorder the block, and the order stays until you reset the layout. Dropped outside the block, the card is pinned where you left it, as before.
- **Command center:** project frames on the phone

  On a phone each project's threads now sit in a dashed frame in the project's colour, as on the desktop.
