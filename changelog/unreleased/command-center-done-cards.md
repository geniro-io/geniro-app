### New

- **Command center:** memory entries, lessons and knowledge changes waiting for your review appear in the manager chat

  Each one arrives by itself as a card with the same buttons Settings offers — approve, forget, save, edit, reject or review — so you can decide it without leaving the chat. More than five of one kind at once arrive as one card. Once decided, here or in Settings, the card stays and says what became of it.

### Improved

- **Command center:** a finished thread's card in the manager chat shows its project, its pull request as a link and its agent's latest report, can be folded away with Dismiss, and appears even when the thread finished while Geniro was closed
- **Command center:** a finished thread's card shows when the thread is archived, with Unarchive, wherever it was archived

### Fixed

- **Command center:** a thread that finishes again no longer adds another Done card to the manager chat
- **Command center:** a finished thread's card, in the manager chat and on the graph, offers Archive once instead of twice, and never shows your own last message as the thread's report
- **Command center:** opening a thread from the manager chat brings its card into view every time, not every other time
- **Knowledge:** a change proposed by an agent of unknown name reads "an agent" rather than "agent · agent"
