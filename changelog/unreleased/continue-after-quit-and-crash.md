### New

- **Team runs:** after a crash, Geniro asks once on start-up whether to continue the team runs it cut off

  When Geniro stops unexpectedly while team runs are working, the next start shows one dialog listing them. Continue all of them, or pick which ones; the rest stay stopped, and writing in one of their threads later continues it too. Agents carry on in the same sessions, calls that were in flight continue in their own conversations, and each agent is told which of its monitors stopped.

### Improved

- Quitting Geniro no longer stops your team runs: they carry on by themselves when you open it again, like after an update
