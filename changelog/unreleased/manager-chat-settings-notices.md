### Fixed

- The Settings cards in the command center's manager chat (updates, sign-ins, shared links, memory conflicts) now appear where they came up in the conversation and scroll with it, instead of sitting at its foot, even when the chat was closed at the time, and **Dismiss** removes a card on every device until something new appears. A paired phone shows the same cards, the storage sign-in included, and the manager's chat button shows how many of them still wait on you.
- A sign-in's progress line keeps its **Open** and **Cancel** buttons on a narrow screen, such as a phone.
- **Dismiss** on a finished thread's card in the manager chat now removes it on every device, and the thread's next finish arrives as a new card.
