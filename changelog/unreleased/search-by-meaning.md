### New

- **Search by meaning:** memory, knowledge and chat search can also find what means the same in other words or in another language

  Turn it on in Settings → Search and Geniro downloads a search model once (about 346 MB) and runs it on your Mac. A Russian question then finds an English note, for your agents' searches and for your own. Your chat history is indexed in the background and waits while the Mac is on battery. Off by default; turning it off keeps the files, and one button deletes them.
