### New

- **Search by meaning:** choose what is indexed, and how far back chats go

  Settings → Search now has a switch each for memory, knowledge and chats, and a window for chats: all time, the last 7, 30 or 90 days, or a number of days you pick. What you leave out drops out of the meaning index and stops using CPU and disk, while word search keeps finding it, and searches say when part of the answer is words only. Everything starts switched on for all time, as before.
